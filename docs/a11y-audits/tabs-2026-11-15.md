## Failures

### Fixed: selected tab label disappeared in forced colors (default and vertical) - was HIGH
Location: `src/components/tabs/Tabs.scss:46,70,150` (`--_active-adjust: none` for default/vertical, `auto` for line; `forced-color-adjust: var(--_active-adjust, auto)` on the active trigger)
Before: computed `HighlightText` on Highlight, but the rendered pill showed an empty white rectangle (Canvas text backplate behind white text) on Default and Vertical.
After: computed `forced-color-adjust` is `none` on the active default and vertical tab; 3x screenshots show a solid highlight pill with readable "Overview" in both; line variant unchanged and visible; the behavior spec asserts none (default) and auto (line); tests/tabs 19/19.

### Fixed: selected vs unselected tab not 3:1 in the default variant, dark mode - was MEDIUM
Location: `spec/components/tabs/contract.json` tokens `tabs.trigger.border-width`, `tabs.trigger.active.border`; `src/components/tabs/Tabs.scss`
Before: dark selected text vs inactive text 2.5:1, fill 1.3:1; no non-color cue.
After: every trigger has a 1px transparent border; the selected default/vertical tab shows `color.input`: `rgb(115,115,115)` vs track `rgb(250,250,250)` 4.54:1 light, vs `rgb(38,38,38)` 3.19:1 dark. Tab height stays 30px for every tab before and after clicking another tab (no layout jump), list heights unchanged (36px horizontal, 96px vertical). Line variant unchanged: transparent border, indicator `rgb(10,10,10)` / `rgb(250,250,250)`. Forced colors: selected pill border is Highlight on Highlight fill (default, vertical, `forced-color-adjust: none`), line keeps `auto`; note the transparent borders of unselected tabs render as visible CanvasText outlines in forced colors (selected stays distinct by fill), not a failure. Contrast pair `tabs.trigger.active.border` vs `tabs.list.bg` added; T6 passes; the spec 'the selected tab has a 3:1 border...' passes.

Also verified: the panel's copied name follows tab text changes where `ariaLabelledByElements` is missing (covered by the spec; AX tree still shows `tabpanel` named by its tab).
