## Differences and action plan
1. `href=""` renders an `<a>` with no href (not a link, not focusable) because `this.href !== null` selects the anchor and `|| nothing` drops the value (Item.ts render). Fix in code: treat empty like absent, or keep the empty href.
2. listitem role is assigned only in `connectedCallback` when the parent is a `sb-item-group` at that moment; moving an item into or out of a group leaves a stale role (Item.ts:19). Fix in code: recompute on reconnect and remove the role it set.
